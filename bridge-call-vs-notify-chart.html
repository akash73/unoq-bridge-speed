Why there's no notify spread chart: Bridge.notify is fire-and-forget — the sketch never replies, so there is nothing to time per call without adding artificial waiting. The notify benchmark instead times one batch of 10,000 back-to-back sends and reports the average (2.362 ms/op). That number is comparable to Bridge.call's mean, but has no min/median/p95/max equivalent.
